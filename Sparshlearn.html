🧣 Learn More About Project Sparsh

A Touch of Warmth for the Unsheltered

By Shyamashree Sitala Maa Charitable Trust

🌟 What is Project Sparsh?

Project Sparsh is a winter humanitarian campaign launched by Shyamashree Sitala Maa Charitable Trust to distribute woolen blankets to unsheltered and homeless individuals.

The word Sparsh (स्पर्श) means “a gentle touch” — and that’s what this project aims to be: a compassionate gesture that brings relief, dignity, and hope to those battling the cold without protection.

👥 Who Will Benefit?

  • Homeless individuals sleeping in the open
  • Elderly people without family support
  • Daily wage workers living in plastic tents
  • Women and children in unprotected shelters
  • Anyone at risk due to lack of warm clothing or bedding
🎯 Goal of the Project: To protect vulnerable and unsheltered individuals from the harsh effects of winter by providing warm woolen blankets, ensuring dignity, safety, and survival for those living without shelter.

📍 Where Will It Happen?

Project Sparsh focuses on unsheltered populations in and around Burdwan District, West Bengal.

Target areas include:

  • Roadside settlements
  • Railway stations and bus terminals
  • Slum pockets
  • Footpaths and open public spaces
  • Remote villages lacking winter support

🤝 How Can You Help?

Whether you're an individual, a company, or an NGO partner — your support can make a direct difference.

  • Donate: ₹250 = 1 blanket = 1 life made warmer
  • In-kind Contribution: New woolen blankets or shawls
  • Sponsor a Batch: Fund 50/100/200 blankets
  • Volunteer: Join us in ground distribution
  • Corporate CSR: Partner with us for visibility and impact
  • Spread the Word: Share our campaign on social media

📸 Transparency & Reporting

  • All donations are acknowledged
  • Distribution will be documented with photos & reports
  • 80G tax benefits available for financial donations
  • Full impact report shared with major donors and CSR partners

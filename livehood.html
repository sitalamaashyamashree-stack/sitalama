🛠️ Featured Livelihood Initiative

Vocational Skill Training & Sustainable Livelihoods

Providing practical vocational and technical training to unemployed youth and low-income families so they can secure dignified employment or launch sustainable micro-enterprises.

  • Job-Oriented Technical & Craft Training: Practical workshops designed around local market demand and self-employment.
  • Micro-Business & Self-Help Support: Mentorship and starter toolkits helping rural families earn steady income.
  • Daily Hunger Relief (Shitala Canteen): Serving nutritious ₹1 evening dinners to daily wage labourers and the homeless.
  • Seasonal Relief (Project Sparsh & Chhaya): Winter blankets, umbrellas, and essential welfare kits for vulnerable households.

Eye Protection & Vision Care Camps

Preventable blindness and untreated refractive errors severely affect elderly villagers, daily wage earners, and school children. Our regular community camps ensure accessible eye protection and clear vision for all.

  • Free Comprehensive Eye Check-up: Vision testing & early cataract detection by qualified optometrists.
  • UV-Protective & Prescription Spectacles: Free eyeglasses distributed on-site to restore daily independence.
  • Cataract Surgery Referral Support: Guided hospital coordination for senior citizens needing critical eye care.
  • Eye Hygiene & Protection Awareness: Preventive care workshops for outdoor workers and students.

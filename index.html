About Us

At Shyamashree Sitala Maa Charitable Trust, we are committed to uplifting underprivileged communities through focused efforts in health, education, and sustainable development. We believe in driving measurable change by organizing grassroots programs that empower individuals and families—especially women and children.

Transparency and accountability guide every initiative we run, ensuring every donation and action makes a visible, lasting impact. Our approach is rooted in trust, collaboration, and results.

Why Support Us?

Your kindness can create lasting change. 💛

✓

100% Transparency –

We maintain clear records of every donation received and how it's used. Regular reports, project photos & updates are shared.

✓

Grassroots Impact –

We work directly in underprivileged communities, ensuring maximum reach with minimum overheads.

✓

Registered & Compliant –

Registered under Indian Registration Act, 1909. Certified for 12AB & 80G. Listed on NITI Aayog Darpan.

✓

Community Trust –

We’re deeply rooted in the communities we serve — trusted, visible, and locally involved.

✓

Volunteer-Led & Impact-Driven –

Driven by passionate volunteers, ensuring measurable results and real change in real communities.

Registered Public Charitable Trust: Registered under the Indian Registration Act, 1909, with valid 12AB & 80G Tax Exemption certificates and listed on NITI Aayog NGO Darpan. Every rupee is audited and receipted.
Direct Grassroots Allocation: Over 90% of contributions go straight toward Shitala Canteen meals, Nirbhoyini women safety kits, Antardrishti braille & white canes, and student scholarships.
Building Self-Reliant Communities: From Howrah across West Bengal, we focus on long-term dignity—combining hunger relief, education, healthcare, and women’s empowerment.

About Us

At Shyamashree Sitala Maa Charitable Trust, we are committed to uplifting underprivileged communities through focused efforts in health, education, and sustainable development. We believe in driving measurable change by organizing grassroots programs that empower individuals and families—especially women and children.

Transparency and accountability guide every initiative we run, ensuring every donation and action makes a visible, lasting impact. Our approach is rooted in trust, collaboration, and results.

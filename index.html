About Us

At Shyamashree Sitala Maa Charitable Trust, we are dedicated to creating meaningful change in underserved communities through initiatives focused on food security, education, healthcare, women's empowerment, and social welfare. We believe that every individual deserves dignity, equal opportunities, and access to essential support. Through grassroots initiatives, we strive to empower individuals and families, particularly women, children, and vulnerable communities.

Transparency, accountability, and compassion are at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to ensuring that every contribution is responsibly utilised and every initiative creates meaningful, measurable impact.

A MESSAGE FROM THE FOUNDER

Founder’s Message

Rooted in Tradition, Committed to Humanity

For over 300 years, Shyamashree Shitala Maa Mandir has carried forward a rich legacy of faith and tradition. Inspired by this heritage, we have been actively involved in social welfare activities for more than 30 years.

To extend our service beyond boundaries of caste, religion, and background, we established Shyamashree Shitala Maa Charitable Trust.

Our vision is to create a society where everyone has access to dignity, opportunity, and support. I believe that every act of kindness, however small, can make a meaningful difference.

With gratitude and commitment, Mr. Shambhu Chakraborty Founder, Shyamashree Shitala Maa Charitable Trust

🌙 Shitala Canteen — Feed with Dignity, Every Night

🍽 A ₹1 Dinner for the Forgotten

Every evening we bring a hot meal to those sleeping and living on the streets — because hunger doesn’t follow a clock.

🤝 Our Mission

To serve one hot, dignified meal every evening to the most neglected people of our society—daily wage workers, beggars, elderly, the homeless, and street-dwelling poor.

📍 No Fixed Place — Because Hunger Moves

Mobile Evening Kitchen

Shitala Canteen is mobile: we go where hunger is found — footpaths, railway platforms, bus stops, local markets, temple premises, slums, and under flyovers. Each evening volunteers serve in a new area where help is needed most.

Typical Spots

  • Footpaths & roadside dwellings
  • Railway platforms & bus stops
  • Local markets & temple premises
  • Slums & road-side shelters

💰 How It Works

₹49 is raised via public donation (80G eligible). The beneficiary contributes a symbolic ₹1. Each ₹50 serves one dinner with dignity and care.

Beneficiary contributions are recorded under “Beneficiary Contribution – Symbolic – Non-80G” in our accounts.

🙏 Sponsor a Day — Feed with Name

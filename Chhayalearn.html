🌂 Project Chhaya

A Shade of Care — A Symbol of Dignity

Organized by Shyamashree Sitala Maa Charitable Trust

🧭 What is Project Chhaya?

A heartfelt initiative to provide free umbrellas to those who live without shelter — individuals who endure the scorching sun, heavy rains, and unpredictable weather every day.

A simple umbrella can offer relief, dignity, and humanity.

🎯 Our Goal

To donate 500+ umbrellas to unsheltered individuals across rural and semi-urban communities.

“No one should have to face the rain or sun without even a shade of protection.”

👥 Who Will Benefit?

  • Homeless individuals living on footpaths
  • Daily wage earners without shelter
  • Elderly in extreme poverty
  • Street vendors & hawkers exposed all day
  • Children walking long distances to school
  • Widows & abandoned women in shelters

📍 Areas of Focus

Phase 1 will cover:

  • Rural villages & roadside settlements
  • Slums and temporary hutments
  • Public places where homeless reside
Starting in: Purba Bardhaman, West Bengal

🫱🏽‍🫲🏼 Why Umbrellas?

  • Low-cost, high-impact aid
  • Instant relief from sun & rain
  • Light to carry, easy to distribute
  • Symbol of protection & dignity

📷 Our Approach

We personally visit target locations, identify vulnerable individuals, and hand over umbrellas with respect. Every distribution is documented transparently.

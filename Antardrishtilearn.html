👁 Project Antardrishti

Inclusive Support for the Visually Impaired

Accessible Education & Independent Mobility for dignity and freedom.

📖 About the Project

Project Antardrishti is an inclusive humanitarian initiative by Shyamashree Shitala Maa Charitable Trust, dedicated to transforming the lives of visually impaired individuals through two core pillars:

  • 📚 Accessible Education via Braille Books
  • 🚶 Independent Mobility with White Canes

We aim to empower vision-impaired individuals with tools to live confident, productive lives.

🌟 Why This Project Matters

India is home to over 12 million visually impaired people—the largest in the world. Most still lack access to aids like Braille textbooks and white canes. Project Antardrishti bridges this gap by:

  • Distributing Braille books tailored to school curricula
  • Providing white canes for safe, independent movement
  • Organizing awareness drives and training camps

🎯 Our Objectives

  • Promote inclusive education for visually impaired students
  • Support mobility independence and confidence
  • Engage schools, parents, and communities
  • Build partnerships with CSR, NGOs, government schemes
  • Maintain transparency with audits and reporting

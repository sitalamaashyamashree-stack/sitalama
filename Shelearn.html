🌿 Women & Adolescent Health Initiative

Project SHE – Sanitary Health Empowerment

(An initiative by Shyamashree Shitala Maa Charitable Trust)

📘 About the Project

Project SHE is our ongoing initiative to empower adolescent girls and women through menstrual hygiene education, access to sanitary products, and health awareness.

Throughout the year, we conduct special camps across different cities and villages of West Bengal, focusing on underprivileged communities.

✨ What Each Camp Provides

  • One-Month Menstrual Hygiene Support – Free Sanitary Napkins
  • Iron & Folic Acid Tablets to fight anaemia
  • Menstrual hygiene awareness session
  • Printed hygiene guides on safe usage and disposal
  • Basic health screening & monitoring

🌿 Our Mission

We believe dignity should not be a privilege. Through education, access, and empathy, we aim to:

  • Reduce school absenteeism among rural girls
  • Improve women’s health by combating anaemia
  • Break taboos and promote safe, dignified practices
